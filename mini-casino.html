      Blackjack

      Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over.

      Card values

      Number cards count as their number. Jacks, Queens and Kings count as 10. An Ace counts as 11, or as 1 if 11 would take you over 21. A hand where an Ace still counts as 11 is called soft.

      How a hand goes

      1. Tap chips to bet: each tap adds that much. Each hand starts with no bet, and Deal again · $50 repeats your last one. The money comes straight from your wallet, and whatever's left goes straight back when the hand ends.
      2. You and the dealer each get two cards. One of the dealer's cards is face down.
      3. On your turn, Hit takes another card, Stand keeps what you have, Double doubles your bet for exactly one more card, and Split turns two cards of the same value into two hands, each with its own bet.
      4. Go over 21 and you bust: that hand loses, whatever the dealer does.
      5. Then the dealer turns over the hidden card and draws until reaching 17 or more.

      Payouts

      ResultPays
      Win: you beat the dealer, or the dealer busts1 to 1
      Blackjack: an Ace and a 10-value card as your first two3 to 2
      Push: a tieBet back
      Insurance, when the dealer has blackjack2 to 1

      1 to 1 means a $10 bet wins $10, so you get $20 back. A $10 blackjack gets $25 back.

      Table rules

      • Six decks, shuffled again when about a quarter of the shoe is left.
      • The dealer stands on all 17s, soft 17 included.
      • Double on any first two cards, including after a split.
      • Split up to four hands. Split Aces get one card each, and 21 after a split counts as 21, not blackjack.
      • When the dealer shows an Ace you're offered insurance. It costs half your bet and pays 2 to 1 if the dealer has blackjack.

      Playing with friends

      From the Blackjack screen, start a room and send the invite link. Up to four players can sit, and anyone with the link can watch. You need to be signed in to take a seat. With two or more players, each turn has a 30-second timer, and betting closes 15 seconds after the first player is ready.

      Tips

      At a table, turn on Hints (top right) and the best move by basic strategy lights up.
      • Always split Aces and 8s. Never split 10s or 5s.
      • Double on 11 unless the dealer shows an Ace, and on 10 when the dealer shows 2 to 9.
      • Stand on 17 or more.
      • With 13 to 16, stand if the dealer shows 2 to 6, and hit otherwise. With 12, stand only against 4 to 6.
      • Skip insurance: over time it costs more than it pays.

      Roulette

      Bet on where the ball will land. This is a single-zero wheel: 37 pockets, numbered 0 to 36.

      How to bet

      1. Tap a spot on the board to highlight it: a single number, or one of the outside bets around them.
      2. Tap chips to bet on the highlighted spot. Tap another spot to bet there instead.
      3. Tap Spin. Winning bets are paid straight into your wallet.

      Tap a chip to bet it; each tap adds that much. Undo takes back your last chip and Clear takes back everything. Every round starts with nothing down, and the main button then offers your last bets again with the amount on it, like Spin again · $50.

      Bets and payouts

      BetCoversPays
      A single number1 number35 to 1
      A dozen: 1st, 2nd or 3rd 1212 numbers2 to 1
      A column (the 2 to 1 spots)12 numbers2 to 1
      Red or black, odd or even, 1–18 or 19–3618 numbers1 to 1

      The zero

      0 is green, and it isn't red, black, odd or even, so every outside bet loses when it comes up. That one pocket is the house's edge: about 2.7% of every bet over time, whichever bets you choose.

      Tips

      • No betting pattern beats the zero, so bet whatever's fun.
      • Outside bets win almost half the time. Single numbers rarely win, but pay big.
      • The recent numbers sit next to the wheel, but every spin is independent of the last.

      Video poker

      You get five cards and one chance to swap any of them. Your final hand pays from the table. There are two machines: Jacks or Better and Deuces Wild. Tap the machine's name above the chips to switch between hands.

      How a hand goes

      1. Tap chips to bet (each tap adds that much), then tap Deal.
      2. Tap the cards you want to keep. They lift up and show Held. Tap one again to let it go.
      3. Tap Draw. Every card you didn't hold is replaced from the rest of the deck.
      4. Your final hand pays from the table at the top of the screen.

      Pay table

      HandPays

      Pays are a multiple of your bet and include it, so Jacks or better just gives your bet back, and a full house on a $10 bet returns $90.

      The hands

      • Royal flush: A, K, Q, J and 10, all the same suit.
      • Straight flush: five in a row, all the same suit.
      • Four of a kind: four cards of the same rank.
      • Full house: three of a kind plus a pair.
      • Flush: five cards of the same suit.
      • Straight: five in a row. An Ace can start one (A-2-3-4-5) or end one (10-J-Q-K-A).
      • Three of a kind and two pair are what they sound like. Jacks or better is a pair of Jacks, Queens, Kings or Aces.

      What to hold

      Go down this list and hold the first thing your hand has.

      1. A royal flush, straight flush or four of a kind
      2. Four cards to a royal flush
      3. A full house, flush, straight or three of a kind
      4. Four cards to a straight flush
      5. Two pair
      6. A pair of Jacks or better
      7. Three cards to a royal flush
      8. Four cards to a flush
      9. A low pair (2s to 10s)
      10. Four cards in a row that could finish at either end
      11. Two high cards (J, Q, K or A) of the same suit
      12. Three cards to a straight flush
      13. Two high cards of different suits
      14. A 10 with a J, Q or K of the same suit
      15. One high card
      16. Nothing: draw five new cards
      Played this way, this pay table gives back about 99.5% of what you bet over time, the best odds in the casino.

      Deuces Wild

      Every 2 is wild: it counts as whatever card makes your hand best, and it's marked Wild on the screen. With four wild cards in the deck, hands come easier, so the lowest paying hand is three of a kind.

      HandPays
      • A royal flush here means one with no 2s in it. A wild royal uses at least one 2.
      • Five of a kind is only possible with wild cards, like three 9s and two 2s.

      What to hold in Deuces Wild

      A simple guide. Always hold every 2, then:

      • Three 2s: hold just the 2s, unless you have a wild royal or five of a kind.
      • Two 2s: hold just the 2s, unless you have four of a kind or better, or four cards to a royal.
      • One 2: keep three of a kind or better. Otherwise hold the 2 with three suited cards toward a royal or straight flush, or just the 2.
      • No 2s: keep any paying hand, but break a straight or flush for four cards to a royal. Otherwise keep a pair (just one, if you have two), then three cards to a royal, four to a flush or four in a row. With nothing, draw five new cards.
      With perfect play this Deuces Wild table gives back about 99.7%, a hair better than Jacks or Better, but it swings more: a lot of the return is in the rare big hands.

      Baccarat

      Bet on which hand finishes closer to 9: the Player's or the Banker's. You don't play either hand. The cards are dealt by fixed rules, so all you choose is your bet.

      How a round goes

      1. Tap Player, Banker or Tie to highlight it, then tap chips to bet on it. You can bet on more than one.
      2. Tap Deal. Each hand gets two cards, sometimes a third, and the one closer to 9 wins.

      Tap a chip to bet it; each tap adds that much. Undo takes back your last chip and Clear takes back everything. Every round starts with nothing down, and the main button then offers your last bets again with the amount on it, like Deal again · $50.

      Counting

      Aces are worth 1, 2 to 9 are worth their number, and 10s and picture cards are worth 0. Only the last digit of the total counts, so a 7 and an 8 make 15, which is 5. The best hand is 9.

      Payouts

      BetPays
      Player1 to 1
      Banker: 1 to 1, less a 5% commission0.95 to 1
      Tie8 to 1

      On a tie, Player and Banker bets come back. A $20 Banker bet that wins returns $39.

      When a third card comes

      • If either hand has 8 or 9 from its first two cards (a natural), nobody draws.
      • Otherwise the Player draws on 0 to 5 and stands on 6 or 7.
      • If the Player stood, the Banker draws on 0 to 5. If the Player drew, the Banker follows this table:
      Banker hasDraws if the Player's third card is
      0, 1 or 2Anything
      3Anything but 8
      42 to 7
      54 to 7
      66 or 7
      7Never

      The odds

      The Banker wins about 45.9% of rounds, the Player 44.6%, and they tie 9.5% of the time. Over time the house keeps about 1.06% of Banker bets, 1.24% of Player bets and 14.4% of Tie bets.

      Tips

      • Banker is the best bet in the game, even after the commission.
      • Tie pays big, but it's the worst bet on the table.
      • The row of results at the top shows what's come up, newest first. It doesn't predict the next round: every round here is dealt from a freshly shuffled 8-deck shoe.

      Three Card Poker

      Your three cards against the dealer's three. Look at your hand, then decide whether it's worth playing.

      How a hand goes

      1. Tap chips to bet on the Ante, which starts highlighted.
      2. For the Pair Plus side bet, tap Pair Plus to highlight it, then tap chips. You can play Pair Plus on its own.
      3. Tap Deal. You get three cards face up, the dealer three face down.
      4. Tap Fold to give up your Ante, or Play to add a Play bet the same size as your Ante.
      5. The dealer's cards turn over and every bet is paid.

      Tap a chip to bet it; each tap adds that much. Undo takes back your last chip and Clear takes back everything. Every round starts with nothing down, and the main button then offers your last bets again with the amount on it, like Deal again · $50.

      Who wins

      • The dealer needs queen high or better to qualify. If not, your Ante wins 1 to 1 and your Play bet comes back.
      • If the dealer qualifies, the better hand wins. You win: Ante and Play both pay 1 to 1. Dealer wins: you lose both. A tie gives both back.

      Hands, best first

      With three cards, a straight beats a flush, because it's harder to get.

      • Straight flush: three in a row, all the same suit.
      • Three of a kind: three cards of the same rank.
      • Straight: three in a row. A-K-Q is the highest; A-2-3 is the lowest.
      • Flush: three of the same suit.
      • Pair, then high card.

      Bonus payouts

      Your handPair PlusAnte bonus
      Straight flush40 to 15 to 1
      Three of a kind30 to 14 to 1
      Straight6 to 11 to 1
      Flush4 to 1–
      Pair1 to 1–

      Pair Plus pays on your hand alone, whatever the dealer has, even if you fold. The Ante bonus is paid whenever you play, even if the dealer beats you.

      When to play

      Play any hand that's Q-6-4 or better: a pair or better, or queen high with a 6 or better second card and a 4 or better third. Fold anything worse.

      Played that way, the house keeps about 3.4% of the Ante over time. It keeps about 2.3% of Pair Plus bets.

      Casino War

      You and the dealer each get one card, and the higher card wins. If they match, you choose: go to war, or surrender.

      How a round goes

      1. Tap chips to bet on War, your main bet, which starts highlighted.
      2. For the Tie side bet, tap Tie to highlight it, then tap chips. It needs a War bet beside it.
      3. Tap Deal. You get a card, then the dealer does. Aces are high and suits don't matter.
      4. Higher card wins 1 to 1. Lower card loses.

      Tap a chip to bet it; each tap adds that much. Undo takes back your last chip and Clear takes back everything. Every round starts with nothing down, and the main button then offers your last bets again with the amount on it, like Deal again · $50.

      When the cards tie

      • Surrender: you get half your War bet back and the round ends.
      • Go to war: you add a raise the same size as your War bet. Three cards are burned, then you each get one more card.
      • If your war card ties or beats the dealer's, the raise wins 1 to 1 and your War bet comes back. If it's lower, you lose both.

      So a $10 bet that goes to war and wins comes out $10 ahead. If it loses the war, it's down $20.

      Payouts

      BetPays
      War, higher card1 to 1
      Raise, war card ties or wins1 to 1
      SurrenderHalf back
      Tie, on the first two cards10 to 1

      The odds

      Every round is dealt from a freshly shuffled 6-deck shoe. The first two cards tie about 7.4% of the time. Once you go to war, your card ties or beats the dealer's about 53.7% of the time.

      Over time the house keeps about 2.9% of War bets if you always go to war, and 3.7% if you always surrender. It keeps about 18.6% of Tie bets.

      Tips

      • Always go to war. Surrendering looks safer, but it costs you more over time.
      • The Tie bet pays big, but it's one of the worst bets in the casino.

      Red Dog

      Two cards go face up. You're betting that the third lands strictly between them. The closer together they are, the more it pays.

      How a hand goes

      1. Tap chips to bet (each tap adds that much), then tap Deal. Each hand starts with no bet; Deal again · $50 repeats your last one.
      2. Two cards come out, lower on the left. Aces are high and suits don't matter.
      3. The spread is how many ranks fit between them. A 4 and a 9 have a spread of 4: 5, 6, 7 and 8.
      4. Tap Raise to add a bet the same size as yours, or No raise. Then the third card comes out.
      5. If it lands in between, your bet and raise win at the spread's odds. If not, you lose them.

      Special hands

      • Back to back, like a 7 and an 8: nothing fits between, so your bet comes back.
      • A pair: a third card comes out right away. If it makes three of a kind, you win 11 to 1. If not, your bet comes back.

      Payouts

      SpreadPays
      15 to 1
      24 to 1
      32 to 1
      4 to 111 to 1
      Pair, then three of a kind11 to 1

      When to raise

      Raise when the spread is 7 or more. Below that the third card misses more often than it lands, even with the bigger payouts on small spreads.

      Every hand is dealt from a freshly shuffled 8-deck shoe. Raising only on 7 or more, the house keeps about 2.8% of your bets over time. Never raising, it keeps about 7.9%.

      Duck Drop plinko

      Drop rubber ducks down a board of pegs and see which bucket they land in. It's in the Arcade, and every duck is paid from your wallet.

      How to play

      1. Pick a chip: that's what each duck costs.
      2. Pick a risk level at the top: Low, Medium or High.
      3. Tap Drop for one duck, or ×10 for ten in a row.
      4. Each duck pays the multiplier on the bucket it lands in.

      Risk levels

      RiskMiddleEdges
      Low0.5×10×
      Medium0.3×27×
      High0.1×100×

      Most ducks land near the middle, so higher risk means more small losses and rarer, much bigger wins. Every level pays back about 97% over time.

      Good to know

      • Where a duck lands comes only from how it bounces. Nothing steers it: the bounces are worked out the moment you drop it, and the fall you see plays them out.
      • If you leave while ducks are still falling, they're paid exactly what they were going to land on.
      • Every peg plays a note when a duck hits it, so turn the sound up.

      Duck Rocket crash

      A rubber duck rides a rocket, and the multiplier climbs as it goes up. Cash out before it blows up to win your bet times the multiplier. Wait too long and you lose the bet. It's in the Arcade, and bets come from your wallet.

      How to play

      1. Pick a chip for your bet, and tap Auto to pick an automatic cash-out (1.5× to 10×, or off).
      2. Tap Launch. After a countdown the rocket lifts off and the multiplier starts at 1.00×.
      3. Tap Cash out whenever you like. Your duck ejects on a parachute, and you win your bet times the multiplier at that moment.
      4. If the rocket explodes before you cash out, you lose the bet.

      Good to know

      • Where the rocket explodes is decided the moment it launches. Nothing you do changes it, so there's no pattern to find.
      • The chance it's still flying at any multiplier is about 0.97 divided by that multiplier: about 48% at 2×, 10% at 10×, 1% at 100×. So whatever you aim for, you get back about 97% over time.
      • Now and then it blows up right on the pad, at 1.00×.
      • After you cash out, the flight speeds up so you can see how far it would have gone.
      • The row of numbers at the top shows where the last rockets exploded.
      • Leaving mid-flight cashes you out at the multiplier at that moment. If the app closes mid-flight, your Auto cash-out still pays if the rocket got that far.
