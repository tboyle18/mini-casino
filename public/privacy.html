Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

Short version: we keep only what the game needs to work: your name, your email and password sign-in if you make an account, and your play-money wallet and stats. No ads today, no tracking or analytics, and we never sell your data.

What we collect

  • Account details (only if you create an account): your name, email address and a password. Passwords are stored hashed by our sign-in provider; we never see them.
  • Game data: your play-money balance and its history, your stats and game saves, and the tables or rooms you create.
  • Guest play: if you play without an account, we give your browser a random guest ID so your wallet comes back next time.
  • Technical data: like any website, our hosting provider handles your IP address and browser details to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

Cookies and storage on your device

  • Sign-in cookies keep you signed in.
  • A guest cookie (mc_guest) remembers your guest wallet.
  • Local storage keeps settings such as sound, your chosen slot machine, the 18+ confirmation and your game stats on this device.

These are all needed for the game to work. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies.

Ads

We don't show ads today. We may later add optional reward ads: after a losing round you could choose to watch a short video ad to win that play money back. Ads would only ever play when you tap to watch one, and you could turn the offers off in Settings. If we add them, the ads would come from an ad provider such as Google, which may use cookies or similar technology to show and measure ads. We'll update this page before ads go live, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

What other players see

Other players see your display name (your first name and last initial, for example "Sam T.") and your results on tables, leaderboards and the big-wins ticker. They never see your email address.

Who processes your data

  • Cloudflare hosts the website and the game server.
  • Neon hosts our database and our sign-in service (Neon Auth).

They process data only to run Mini Casino for us. We don't share or sell your data to anyone else, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account or guest wallet. When you delete your account, your account, sign-in sessions, wallet, stats and saves are deleted from our database straight away.

Deleting your data

Open your account card in the app (Profile, then Delete account) and confirm. This works for guest games too. It can't be undone. You can also clear this site's data in your browser settings to remove what's stored on your device.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your data, or object to how we use it. To ask, contact us below.

Children

Mini Casino is for adults 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you think a child has made an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
